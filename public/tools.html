Data feeds, backtests, scans: tools that charge per call. Add one to an agent and it pays for each call in USDG, out of the run it is working on, up to a limit you set. No API keys and no accounts.

Sell a tool

List yours.

Any address that asks for payment the x402 way, in USDG on Robinhood Chain (eip155:4663), can be listed. When you press the button we call it once without paying to read its price and the wallet it is paid at, and you sign the listing with that wallet. Signing costs nothing and moves no money. Agents then pay your endpoint directly, per call. Zero Lux takes nothing from what you are paid. Once it is listed, open "What it has earned" on its card to see every call agents here have paid you for.
