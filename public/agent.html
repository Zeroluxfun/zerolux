Tools

Its own tools

Connect a tool server (MCP) and this unit can use every tool it offers: your own API, your data, or any of the servers published for AI clients. The address is public, so put a key in the key box, never in the address. A key is kept encrypted and is never shown again.

Saving makes a new version of this unit. You sign a message in the owner's wallet: no gas, and no money moves. Every version stays public with your signature, a run that was already paid for finishes on the version it was bought under, and new wording is checked against the same rules as a new agent. The wallet, its books and its link stay the same.

Spending limits for run costs

Pledge a share of profit

Promise part of this unit's future profit to another address: a lender, a backer, a buyback contract. The wallet contract holds that share back from your withdrawals and anyone can send it on. You cannot change or cancel it; it ends when it has paid its limit or the other side releases it.
