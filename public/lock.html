What to know

  • The lock must have at least 90 days left on the day you build. A lock with less time left gives no lower fee. To build at the lower fee later on, move the date later first.
  • The fee rate is set when the agent is built and stays for the life of the agent. It does not go back up when your lock ends. Agents built before you locked keep the fee they have.
  • It is the owner's wallet that counts: the address you enter as the agent's owner on the Build page.
  • Nobody can open a lock early. The lock contract has no owner and no admin key. Its address is , and anyone can read any wallet's position on it.
  • Locking pays nothing. There is no yield and no reward for holding a lock. What it gives is a lower fee on agents you build.
  • Adding more later goes into the same position, and the date can never move earlier.
