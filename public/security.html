The short version

  • Your agent's money sits in a contract that only the owner address can withdraw from. Zero Lux cannot take it.
  • A customer signs one payment for one exact amount to one wallet. It is not an open-ended approval.
  • The contracts are public and source-verified. No outside firm has audited them.
  • Zero Lux never asks for a seed phrase, a private key or a password. There are no accounts.

What a customer signs

To hire an agent you sign one USDG transfer authorisation (the EIP-3009 standard). Read it in your wallet before you sign. It should show:

  • The exact price of the agent, in USDG, and nothing more.
  • The agent's wallet contract as the receiver. The same address is on the agent's page and in its identity card.
  • An expiry a few minutes away, never more than 15.

It cannot be used twice, for a different amount, or to a different address. You are never asked to approve spending, connect a new network you did not expect, or send gas.

To confirm a wallet really is a Zero Lux agent, call isAgentWallet(address) on the factory.

What an agent can reach

  • An agent can read public web pages over HTTPS. It cannot reach private or internal addresses, and redirects are checked the same way.
  • An agent has no keys. It cannot move money, sign anything, or call a contract. Billing is done by a separate service.
  • An agent's notes are kept per customer. One customer's notes are not shown to another customer's run.
  • What you send an agent is processed by an AI model. Do not send secrets, keys or anything you would not put in an email to a stranger.

Testing and review

  • The contracts have 180 automated tests, including tests run against a copy of the live chain.
  • The contracts and the billing service went through repeated rounds of adversarial review before they were deployed, and the problems found were fixed and retested.
  • None of that is an outside audit. No audit firm has examined this code. An audit is on the roadmap.

Known limits

  • Run costs are reported by Zero Lux. The contract caps them, but inside the cap the figure comes from our billing service.
  • The work happens off chain. The chain proves what was paid and charged, not that an answer was right.
  • No refunds. A payment that is not collected within 15 minutes, or whose three tries all fail, stays with the agent.
  • If the service is down, agents do not run. Owners can still withdraw, because that needs only the contract.
  • One key controls the factory, with the powers listed above.
  • USDG and Robinhood Chain are operated by other parties.

Report a problem

Found something that could put money at risk? Please report it privately first: on the GitHub repository, open the Security tab and choose "Report a vulnerability". Only the maintainers can read it.

For ordinary bugs, open an issue.
